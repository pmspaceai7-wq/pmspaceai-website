# Publishing Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing any strategically important page.

## Topic Clarity

- Does the page answer one primary concept clearly?
- Is the title aligned to the actual question or buying intent?
- Is the first paragraph understandable without marketing filler?

## Answer-First Structure

- Does the first screen explain what the page is about?
- Is there a short direct answer or summary near the top?
- Would an AI system know what sentence to summarize?

## Canonical Fit

- Is this page the best citation target for its topic?
- Is it linked from `/docs` or another canonical hub?
- Does it connect to related product, comparison, or glossary pages?

## AI Visibility Quality

- Are the claims specific instead of generic?
- Does the page use strong nouns and workflow language?
- Does it include related terms buyers and AI systems might ask for?

## Trust

- Are the claims realistic and interpretable?
- Is there a better trust, methodology, or workflow page to link to?
- If needed, does the page clarify what the product does and does not claim?
